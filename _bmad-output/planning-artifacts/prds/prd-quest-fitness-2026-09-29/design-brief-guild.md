# Design brief — Гильдия → product UI

Source: design subagent review of rebranding media + customer constraints.
Status: direction for later `bmad-ux` / PRD addendum. Not a full UX spec.

## Brand in product
- Sign (dragon-in-kettlebell): app icon + header only (24–32 px, often monochrome)
- Quest language in titles/empty states, not on action buttons
- No heavy fantasy illustrations inside product screens
- Rubik for all UI; logo lettering separate

## Color in product
- Product bg: `#FAF7F4`; cards white; text `#241C20`
- Red `#C33F46`: one active accent in viewport (primary CTA, current set, timer)
- Apricot `#EFB68C`: badges/progress tracks, never text
- Cream `#F6EBDD`: onboarding / empty / completion moments
- Live workout: dark charcoal default for readability in gym

## Anti–AI-slop
No mesh gradients, glassmorphism, purple neon, glow, emoji-as-icons, hero card grids.
Fantasy via words + one diamond accent + real gym photos.

## Live workout UI principles
One job per screen; 56–64px bottom CTA; one-hand sweaty fingers; stable layout; no modal interruptions.
